    How it works

    The clock rings, not the phone.

    Alarms are stored on the clock and it rings on its own. Your phone can be dead, muted, or across the room. It has a battery inside, so pulling the plug sends it straight to full volume.

    The app is the only key.

    When it rings, the clock makes up a fresh random challenge. Only the paired app can answer it, and only after you solve its puzzles. The answer is an HMAC proof, so a replay or a fake app gets nowhere.

    Delete the app, it keeps ringing.

    The key lives in the app's storage. Delete the app and the key is gone, but the alarms are not. To shut it up you reinstall, hold the perch to re-pair, and pay a penalty round. The clock logs it as a betrayal.

    Prove you're actually up.

    Five minutes after you silence it, the crow chirps for a wake check. Miss it and the whole thing starts over, louder. If your phone is lost, an SOS pin on the clock gives you a slow, awake-only way out.
